About TV Media

TV Media is an independent digital communications, marketing, and events studio based in Boxborough, MA, focused on helping small businesses grow and support the local communities they serve. We specialize in hands-on social media management, digital & traditional marketing strategy, custom content creation, and event coordination.

We started this company to support local voices across Boxborough, Acton, Littleton, MetroWest, and Central Massachusetts. We know that showing up consistently online while running a small business can feel overwhelming. Our job is to cut through the noise and ensure that your voice is heard, your business is seen, and your story is shared with clarity.

For us, it's not just about posting content. It's about making media matter.
